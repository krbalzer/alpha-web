The practice she built

Started in one room in Denver. Renamed for prevention. Built to go further.

Preva opened on January 1, 2022 as Skin Den, one 100 square foot room in Denver, staffed by people hired for their energy as much as their skill, several of them from the fitness world. That May, Michelle renamed it for what it stands for: preventative aesthetics, natural results, and a name that could travel.

Today it runs in Denver and in Encinitas, opened largely by Denver team members who moved west. New injectors train for at least two to three months before their first basic treatment, and every opening draws more than 300 applications.

Michelle was a nurse and nurse practitioner in the cardiac ICU before she moved into aesthetics in 2019. Her first job trained her for four days and left her with no mentor in the state, so she went looking for her own.

That gap is why Preva trains the way it does. She leads from a wellness-first view of aesthetics and a belief that, with the right mindset, systems and culture, her team can build whatever they set out to. She calls mentoring them the most fulfilling part of everything she has done.
