Site-wide component

Persistent player

One audio element for the whole site. It never reloads, so playback never stops.

01 / AppearsOn first play, anywhere

Any play button on the site loads that episode. The bar slides in above the nav and stays pinned while scrolling.

02 / PersistsPages swap, audio doesn't

Links load the next page into the content area only. The bar sits outside it, so the audio element survives every navigation.

03 / Steps asideOn its own episode page

When the page matches what is playing, the bar hides and the page player takes over the same audio. Any other episode page keeps the bar.

04 / RemembersAcross visits

Episode and position save locally. Next visit shows the bar paused at the same second with a Resume button.
