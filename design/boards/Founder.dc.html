01 / Who she is

She was trained for four days. Her injectors train for months.

Michelle Paty was a nurse and nurse practitioner in the cardiac ICU before she moved into aesthetics in 2019. Her first job trained her for four days, then put her on cheek and jawline filler with no mentor in the state. She learned by doing, and by seeking out injectors she respected.

On January 1, 2022 she opened Skin Den in a 100 square foot room in Denver, hiring for energy as much as skill. That May it became Preva, for preventative aesthetics. Today her injectors train for at least two to three months before their first basic treatment, and every opening draws more than 300 applicants.
